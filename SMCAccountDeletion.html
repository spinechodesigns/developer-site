Deleting your SMC account and data SMC doesn't run its own servers, and its developer never receives your data. Your data lives on your phone and in Google Drive folders shared with the people in your SMCs. To delete everything: in SMC, tap the account icon on the home screen, choose Delete my data, and confirm. SMC will: remove your calendar data from every SMC you've joined, and delete any SMCs you created remove SMC's access to your Google account erase all SMC data from your phone Note: shared events you added to other people's SMCs may stay visible to those members. Need help? Email spinechodesigns@gmail.com from the Google account you use with SMC, with the subject "SMC data deletion". We'll reply within 30 days.
