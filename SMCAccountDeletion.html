Delete your SMC data

SMC – Simple Merged Calendars

SMC doesn't run its own servers, and its developer never receives your data. Your data lives on your phone and in Google Drive folders shared with the people in your SMCs.

Delete everything from the app

  1. Open SMC.
  2. On the home screen, tap the account icon in the top right.
  3. Tap Delete my data, then Delete.

SMC will then:

  • remove your calendar data from every SMC you've joined, and delete any SMCs you created
  • remove SMC's access to your Google account
  • erase all SMC data from your phone

What is deleted

Your name, email address, chosen color, and the calendar events you shared through SMC. Nothing is kept by the developer, because the developer never had a copy.

What may remain

Shared events you added to SMCs owned by other people may stay visible to those members until the SMC's owner deletes it.

Can't open the app?

You can still remove SMC's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete any folders named "SMC - …" from your Google Drive. Uninstalling the app erases its data from your phone.

Need help?

Email spinechodesigns@gmail.com from the Google account you use with SMC, with the subject "SMC data deletion". We'll reply within 30 days.
