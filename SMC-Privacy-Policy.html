Privacy Policy

SMC – Simple Merged Calendars · Effective October 29, 2026

In short: SMC has no servers of its own, and its developer never receives your data. Your calendar is stored on your phone and shared, through Google Drive, only with the people in SMCs you create or join. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device information.

What SMC uses and why

DataWhyWhere it goes
Your name and email address (from your Google account)To identify you to other members of your SMCsYour phone, and the shared Google Drive folder of each SMC you're in
Calendar events from the Google calendars you chooseTo show them in the merged calendarYour phone, and the shared Google Drive folder of each SMC you share them with
Events you create inside an SMC, and your chosen colorTo show them to the SMC's membersSame as above
Email addresses you inviteTo share the SMC's Drive folder with themGoogle Drive, which emails them the invitation

You choose which calendars to share, per SMC, and can turn off Share event details so others only see when you're busy. You can stop syncing an SMC at any time.

Permissions

  • Calendar (read only): to read the calendars you choose to share. SMC never changes your Google calendars.
  • Google Drive: to create SMC folders, and to read and write SMC files in folders shared with you. SMC only works with its own "SMC - …" folders and files.
  • Contacts: SMC doesn't request contacts permission. If you pick someone from your contacts, Android gives SMC only the one email address you selected.

Google user data

SMC's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide SMC's calendar-sharing features. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to train AI models, and not read by people except at your request for support.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows a small banner from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location (based on IP address), app interactions, and diagnostic information, and use them for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Your calendar data is never shared with AdMob. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. SMC Pro has no ads.

Purchases

SMC Pro is bought through Google Play. Google handles payment; SMC only checks whether you own Pro. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Security

All data sent by SMC travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Data stored on your phone is kept in SMC's private app storage. Data in Google Drive is protected by your Google account and shared only with the SMC's members.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you remove it. You can leave any SMC, delete SMCs you created, or use Delete my data in the app to remove everything at once. Full instructions are on the data deletion page.

Children

SMC isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
